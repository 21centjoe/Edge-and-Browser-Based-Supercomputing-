One job is divided into 60 shards, one per C60 vertex, run across your CPU cores, then derived into a single answer and checked against known values.

All nodes here are local Web Workers. A standalone page cannot reach outside machines such as observatory clocks or other clusters; that would need a server you run. Timing uses your browser's timer, which browsers deliberately round to tens of microseconds or coarser.
